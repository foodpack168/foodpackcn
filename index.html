Frequently Asked Questions

Ordering, sampling, customization and compliance — the questions importers ask us most often.

Are you a manufacturer or a trading company?

We are a manufacturer. Shenzhen Food Pack Technology Co., Ltd. runs moulding, printing and assembly in its own workshop in Bao'an District, Shenzhen, and exports directly to distributors, restaurant chains and retail brands.

Can you customize size, colour, structure and printing?

Yes — OEM and ODM are both supported. We customize dimensions, cavity structure, thickness, colour and printing. Your logo can be embossed into the tray wall, printed on IML film, or applied as a private-label sticker.

What is the minimum order quantity?

MOQ depends on the mould and the print method. Trial orders are accepted at a low MOQ — send us the product spec and your target quantity and we will confirm the exact figure for each item.

How long does sampling take, and what does tooling cost?

Sampling takes 3–5 days. Send us drawings, reference samples or photos of the product you have in mind; our engineering team will quote the tooling cost before we start cutting tooling.

Which food-contact certifications do your products pass?

Key products are tested by SGS against US FDA 21 CFR 177.1640 and EU Regulation (EC) No 1935/2004 with (EU) No 10/2011 — all reports are Pass. LFGB reports for the paper series and heavy-metal screening are available on request.

Which materials do you use, and are they food-safe?

Food-grade plastics — PP, PET and PS — plus grease-resistant kraft paper for the paper series. Microwave-safe, freezer-grade and oil-resistant variants are available. Every batch passes incoming-material checks, in-line inspection and a pre-shipment inspection.

Are the kraft paper products really oil resistant?

The kraft paper used in our lunch boxes and soup bowls carries a food-contact grease-resistant coating, so it handles oily and hot food far better than uncoated paper. For very greasy items such as fried food, tell us the application and we will recommend the right grade and thickness.

Which countries do you export to?

We currently export to Spain, Belgium, Peru, Canada, Australia, Hong Kong SAR (China) and the Chinese domestic market. Documents such as packing lists, certificates of origin and inspection reports are prepared for every shipment.

What are your payment and shipping terms?

T/T and L/C at sight are standard; PayPal and Western Union are accepted for sample orders. We quote FOB Shenzhen, and loading is handled by a fixed, trained team with a long-term partner fleet.
