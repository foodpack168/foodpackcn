Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

This policy explains how Shenzhen Food Pack Technology Co., Ltd. (brand: foodpack, "we", "us") handles the personal data you send us through the quote form on this website. We keep it deliberately short and specific: we collect the minimum needed to answer your inquiry and prepare a quotation.

1. What we collect

Only the fields you fill in on the quote form:

  • Name
  • Company name
  • Email address
  • Product of interest
  • Target market
  • Quantity and any message you write

We do not ask for payment details, and we do not run advertising cookies or trackers on this site.

2. Why we use it

To reply to your inquiry, prepare a quotation, and — if you become a customer — to fulfil the order. The legal bases under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) are your consent given by submitting the form (Art. 6(1)(a)), steps taken at your request prior to a contract (Art. 6(1)(b)), and our legitimate interest in answering business inquiries (Art. 6(1)(f)).

We do not sell, rent or share your data with anyone else, and we do not add you to a mailing list.

4. How long we keep it

Inquiries that do not lead to an order are deleted after 24 months. Where a business relationship follows, the correspondence is kept for as long as needed for accounting and warranty purposes under applicable law.

5. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

  • ask for a copy of the data we hold about you (access)
  • have inaccurate data corrected (rectification)
  • have your data erased (erasure)
  • ask us to restrict processing (restriction)
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest (objection)
  • receive your data in a portable format (portability)
  • lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these, email foodpack@foodpackcn.com. We answer within 30 days.

6. Cookies and analytics

This website currently sets no cookies of its own and runs no analytics or advertising scripts. If we add such tools in future, this page will be updated before they go live, and any consent requirement will be handled at that point.
